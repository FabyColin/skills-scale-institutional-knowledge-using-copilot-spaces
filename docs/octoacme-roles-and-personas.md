# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Executive Sponsor provides strategic direction, secures funding and alignment, and removes organizational barriers that could block delivery. This role ensures the initiative remains connected to business outcomes and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Align the project with strategic priorities and business goals
- Approve major scope, funding, and resourcing decisions
- Sponsor cross-functional escalation when risks or trade-offs need executive intervention
- Review progress against value targets and outcomes
- Support executive communication for critical milestones and launch decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm business value and prioritize investment
- Partners with Project Managers to review status, risks, and major decisions
- Supports Developers and delivery teams by clearing dependencies and governance issues
- Uses Product and Project Manager updates to make informed decisions without overshadowing day-to-day execution

### Goals
- Keep the project aligned with business strategy
- Reduce delays caused by unclear authority or resourcing gaps
- Ensure accountability for outcomes at the leadership level

---

## Product Owner / Business Decision Lead

### Role Summary
The Product Owner or Business Decision Lead translates customer and business needs into clear priorities and trade-offs. This role keeps the team focused on the highest-value work and ensures product decisions remain measurable and outcome-driven.

### Responsibilities
- Define priorities and decision criteria for roadmap and feature work
- Clarify scope, backlog ordering, and acceptance expectations
- Resolve business trade-offs when timelines, scope, or resources change
- Validate that delivered work meets customer and stakeholder needs
- Coordinate with Product Managers and stakeholders on business feedback

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates closely with Product Managers to translate strategy into backlog priorities
- Works with Project Managers to align schedule, dependencies, and release commitments
- Provides clarity to Developers on features, scope, and acceptance criteria
- Escalates business blockers to the Executive Sponsor when strategic direction or investment is required

### Goals
- Maintain a clear, prioritized path to customer and business value
- Reduce confusion about scope and expected outcomes
- Improve decision-making across product, delivery, and stakeholders

---

## Delivery Manager / Team Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Delivery Manager or Team Coordinator helps the project run smoothly by managing execution flow, dependency tracking, and team operating rhythm. This role reduces friction between planning and delivery so teams can focus on the work.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate sprint or delivery cadence across teams and workstreams
- Track dependencies, bottlenecks, and resourcing constraints
- Support planning, standups, and recovery meetings after schedule disruptions
- Help maintain delivery health metrics and team communication flow
- Facilitate handoffs between functions such as engineering, product, and operations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers on planning, risk mitigation, and delivery reporting
- Supports Product Managers and Product Owners in backlog flow and priority management
- Helps Developers remove operational blockers and improve throughput
- Provides escalation support to the Executive Sponsor when cross-team issues require leadership attention

### Goals
- Improve predictability and delivery flow
- Reduce delays caused by unresolved dependencies or unclear ownership
- Create smoother collaboration across teams and functions

---

## Risk and Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
The Risk and Compliance Partner identifies operational, legal, and delivery risks that may affect project outcomes. This role supports early warning and decision-making so the team can address issues before they become major blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and document risks, assumptions, and compliance obligations
- Track severity, ownership, mitigation actions, and follow-up timelines
- Partner with Project Managers and stakeholders to review risk exposure
- Ensure the project follows relevant controls, standards, and escalation procedures
- Support issue triage for critical risks or contractual obligations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to monitor risk registers and escalation paths
- Helps Product Managers evaluate trade-offs related to user impact, compliance, or business risk
- Provides guidance to Developers and delivery leads on quality, security, and operational constraints
- Escalates major unresolved risks to the Executive Sponsor for leadership support

### Goals
- Improve early risk identification and response
- Protect delivery outcomes from avoidable operational or compliance issues
- Maintain confidence in project quality and governance

---

## Change Enablement / Communication Lead

### Role Summary
The Change Enablement or Communication Lead helps stakeholders understand what is changing, why it matters, and how to adopt it successfully. This role keeps communication aligned with delivery progress and reduces confusion during transitions.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate stakeholder communications, milestones, and launch updates
- Prepare change impact summaries and adoption guidance
- Support readiness activities for new processes, tools, or release events
- Gather stakeholder feedback and route issues to the appropriate lead
- Help align messaging across product, delivery, and leadership teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to communicate value and user impact
- Works with Project Managers and Delivery Managers to align updates with project milestones
- Supports Developers and Quality leads by translating technical changes into stakeholder-ready communication
- Escalates unresolved stakeholder concerns to the Executive Sponsor when broader alignment is needed

### Goals
- Increase stakeholder clarity and confidence
- Reduce confusion during launches and transitions
- Improve adoption and business readiness for change

---

## Quality / Release Readiness Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality or Release Readiness Lead ensures that the work is ready to ship, validate, and support. This role focuses on quality gates, release confidence, and operational readiness across the final stages of delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define and review release criteria, quality checks, and readiness reviews
- Coordinate validation activities such as testing, verification, and sign-off
- Identify release blockers, operational risks, and fallback needs
- Support communication around deployment readiness and post-release monitoring
- Work with project and delivery leads to reduce surprise at release time

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers on quality standards, testing, and rollout readiness
- Uses Product Manager and Product Owner input to confirm expected outcomes and acceptance thresholds
- Works with Project Managers to align release timing and communication plans
- Escalates unresolved release concerns to the Executive Sponsor when business risk or momentum is affected

### Goals
- Increase confidence in release quality and readiness
- Reduce production issues caused by incomplete validation or unclear handoff
- Improve the consistency of successful rollout and support outcomes

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional personas complement the existing Developer, Product Manager, and Project Manager roles by clarifying accountability, escalation paths, and handoffs across the project lifecycle.
